Captain Vikram Batra

“Yeh Dil Maange More!”

About

Captain Vikram Batra was a brave officer of the Indian Army who played a key role in the Kargil War of 1999. He became a symbol of courage and patriotism for the entire nation.

Achievements

He successfully led missions to capture strategic points in Kargil under extreme conditions. For his extraordinary bravery, he was awarded the Param Vir Chakra, India's highest military honor.

Legacy

Captain Vikram Batra's sacrifice continues to inspire millions of Indians. His fearless attitude and love for the nation make him a true hero forever remembered in history.
